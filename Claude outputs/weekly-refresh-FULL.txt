STEP 2b — USD/ILS FALLBACK REBASE, MONTHLY (Gal, 19/08/2026). Run every week:
  python3 "iPad Dashboards/_regen/rebase_fx.py"
**It no-ops unless the calendar month has changed since the last recorded point**, which is what
makes it monthly without needing a monthly task. Nothing to decide most weeks; just run it.
WHY IT EXISTS. Every page fetches USD/ILS live at load time, and every page also carries a
HARDCODED fallback for when that fetch fails. The live path was maintained and the fallbacks were
not. On 19/08/2026 crypto sat at 3.15 against a live 2.97475 — **5.9% out** — while the other four
sat at 2.9551. A stale fallback does not look broken: it silently prices a whole page at an old
rate the moment the network hiccups, and crypto disagreed with the other four by 6% whenever that
happened. The script rebases all five onto the live rate and appends one dated point to
`Projects/Master project/memory/fx-history.json`, so drift becomes visible instead of silent.
If it rewrote anything it prints `rebased <page> <old> -> <new>`. Those artifacts must then be
mirrored to `Projects/Master project/` and rebuilt in STEP 4 like any other change — the script
does not build. `--check` reports drift and changes nothing (exit 1 if any page is stale).
WHAT THIS IS NOT. It does not give historical per-trade FX. The realized trades in the stock
artifact carry no buy- or sell-date rate, so the shekel gain Israeli CGT is actually assessed on
still cannot be computed for them — see `backlog-2026-08-19.md` §2, still awaiting Gal's call.
This builds that series going FORWARD, one point a month, so there is real history to price
against later. Do not let the existence of fx-history.json be mistaken for backfilled history.
